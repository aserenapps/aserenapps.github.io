Saltella Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Saltella app ("the app") uses, why, and the choices you have. The app is made by Aseren ("we", "us"). You can contact us at mobileprg@gmail.com.

The app has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location.

Information stored on your device

To keep your progress, the app saves the following only on your device: the stages you have cleared, your stars, best times and collectibles found, the character you chose, which tips you have already seen, your settings (such as theme, language, sound, vibration and accessibility options), and counters that limit how often ads appear. We cannot see this information.

If backup is turned on for your Android device, Android may include this information in your device backup to your Google account, so your progress can return after you reinstall. This is managed by Google under your Google account settings.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your device settings, deletes this information from your device.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads never interrupt a run: after a fall you can choose to watch an ad to carry on from where you fell, and an ad may sometimes appear after a run ends. Ads are limited to family-friendly content (rated PG).

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location can be inferred), device type and operating system, and how you interact with ads. This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us.

Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent form before ads are requested. Where Google offers it, you can change your choice at any time with the ad privacy option in the app's settings. Without consent to personalised ads, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which use more limited information.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings, under Privacy > Ads.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Usage statistics

The app can send anonymous gameplay statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase, so we can see which stages are played and improve the game. These include events such as starting or finishing a stage, how it ended, the time taken, the character used, the row reached, using Rewind, a revive or a checkpoint, using an in-game device, and whether an ad was offered or watched. They never include anything that identifies you by name, and the app sets no user ID.

Google Analytics also uses an app instance identifier and records basic device information, such as device model, operating system version and approximate location derived from your IP address. The app tells Google Analytics not to use this information for advertising.

Statistics are only collected where no consent is required, or after you agree. You can turn them off at any time with the usage statistics switch in the app's settings, and your choice is always respected. Statistics are kept for no longer than 14 months.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the information Google collects through the advertising and statistics services described above, the app does not share information with anyone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Security

Information sent to Google's advertising and statistics services is encrypted in transit. Information saved on your device is protected by your device's own security.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, or to object to how it is used. Because we do not hold information that identifies you, most of these choices are available directly in the app and your device settings, as described above. For anything else, or for information held by Google, contact us at mobileprg@gmail.com and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts using information in a new way, we will update this page and the date at the top.
