Aseren Sudoku Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Aseren Sudoku app ("the app") uses, why, and the choices you have. The app is made by Aseren ("we", "us"). You can contact us at aserensudoku@gmail.com.

The app has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

Information stored on your device

To keep your progress, the app saves the following only on your device: your game in progress, your settings (such as theme, language and sound), your best scores and puzzle counts, whether you finished the tutorial, and counters that limit how often ads and rating requests appear. We cannot see this information.

If backup is turned on for your Android device, Android may include this information in your device backup to your Google account, so your progress can return after you reinstall. This is managed by Google under your Google account settings.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your device settings, deletes this information from your device.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads never appear during play: you can choose to watch an ad for an extra hint, and an ad may sometimes appear between puzzles.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location can be inferred), device type and operating system, and how you interact with ads. This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us.

Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with Ad privacy choices in the app's settings. Without consent, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which use more limited information.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings, under Privacy > Ads.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Usage statistics

The app can send anonymous gameplay statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase, so we can see which features are used and improve the game. These include events such as starting or finishing a puzzle, the difficulty, score, time taken, mistakes and hints used, tutorial progress and whether an ad was watched. They never include the contents of your puzzles or anything that identifies you by name.

Google Analytics also uses an app instance identifier and records basic device information, such as device model, operating system version and approximate location derived from your IP address.

Statistics are only collected where no consent is required, or after you agree. You can turn them off at any time with Share usage statistics in the app's settings, and your choice is always respected. Statistics are kept for no longer than 14 months.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the information Google collects through the advertising and statistics services described above, the app does not share information with anyone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Security

Information sent to Google's advertising and statistics services is encrypted in transit. Information saved on your device is protected by your device's own security.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, or to object to how it is used. Because we do not hold information that identifies you, most of these choices are available directly in the app and your device settings, as described above. For anything else, or for information held by Google, contact us at aserensudoku@gmail.com and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts using information in a new way, we will update this page and the date at the top.
